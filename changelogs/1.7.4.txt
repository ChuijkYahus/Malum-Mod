# Technical Changes

Spirit Types
- Spirit Types are now a Deferred Registry
- This is a significant internal change that ideally should not affect gameplay whatsoever
- However this does mean that spirit registry names now come with a mod identifier in addition to a name
- Any third party compat will need to be updated accordingly

Spirit Rites
- Spirit Rites are now a Deferred Registry
- This is a significant internal change that ideally should not affect gameplay whatsoever
- Any addon, such as Gaze which adds more totem rites will need to be updated accordingly

Recipes
- Updated the formats for most recipes added by Malum
- Any third party recipe compat needs to be updated accordingly

KubeJS
- Added KubeJS compat for all Malum recipes
- Check the wiki for example use cases, if the appropriate code snippets are not there remind me to add them
- The Runic Workbench Recipes are still a little weird with KubeJS, this is an issue on their side from what I understand

# New Stuff & Tweaks

Added the Ravenous Pouch
- The Ravnous Pouch will snatch up any item that it already stores
- Offers 8 stacks worth of space, but does not store magical items more efficiently

Soulwoven Pouch
- Based on Community Feedback, the autocollection of a large amount of goods was rather undesirable as it no longer allowed for a dedicated early game spirit storage option
- The Soulwoven Pouch now only automatically collects spirits, but still stores most malum content extra effectively

Runic Workbench
- The Runic Workbench now has a new Model, Sounds, and Visuals for crafting
- The Recipe is also changed to better reflect it's new look
- Runic Workbench Recipes can now support regular items with a secondary input
 - Currently this behavior lacks a unique crafting animation, if any modpack dev ends up using this functionality ping me and I'll whip something up

Soulwoven Banners
- Added one new Soulwoven Banner Pattern
- Soulwoven Banners now have a new particle effect for being Imbued with a Spirit
- Soulwoven Banners now have a new particle effect for being Sheared

Waveform Artifice
- Polished up the Internal Logic of all the Waveform Diodes
- Changed the Sound used by the Diodes

Soul Stained Steel Armor
- The Soul Stained Steel Armor now has a new model

Malignant Stronghold Armor
- The Malignant Stronghold Armor now has a new model
- The Malignant Stronghold Chestplate now comes with two scarves instead of one
- Pride Scarves retain old functionality
- No Longer Eats Iron's Spellbooks Magic Resistances

Malignant Conversion
- No longer grants Armor Toughness
- No longer grants Magic Resistance
- Now grants a new attribute called Malignant Reinforcement
- Malignant Reinforcement grants a chance to completely resist any incoming damage

Necklace of the Hidden Blade
- Now always deals a minimum of 10 damage
- Damage Bonus based on damage taken scales slightly faster, for each heart and a half it will add 15% damage, this used to be 20% but with a higher health requirement
- No Longer Damages Armor
- This should hopefully enable the trinket to feel more rewarding on it's own while still allowing for some crazy strength

Sundering Anchor
- The Sundering Anchor now has reduced throw cooldown if it did not hit any enemies
- The Sundering Anchor Projectile will no longer target Tamed Animals
- The Sundering Anchor Projectile will not target Players that do not already have the Hatred effect applied, unless Players are the only available target

Pact of the Prospector
- Originally Pact of the Flamekeeper
- Updated Behavior
- Now Grants stacks of Prospector's Steak when Collecting Resources from Mined Ores
- Prospector's Steak grants Fortune Change and Mining Speed
- Taking Damage Removes Prospector's Streak and Ignites You
- Added VFX to Ignition Effect

This Geas was the last one I added before the update and as such it felt a little rushed.
I think it lacked a clear identity, Fortune Chance was a rather unexplored attribute in Malum and I figured it'd make a great addition here
It's name matches the Belt of the Prospector 

Pact of the Profane Glutton
- Updated Behavior
- Gluttony becomes Desperate Need
- Desperate Need is consumed when attacking with a scythe to trigger a Poisonous Slash
- Poisonous Slashes apply Poison proportional to the amount of Desperate Need stacks
- Taking Poison Damage Spreads the Poison to you
- Taking Hunger Damage Poisons You

I wasn't too happy with the execution of this geas, I think this change in behavior really helps it
It's meant to serve as an aggressive glass cannon type deal, before this change it lacked a visual representation for your growth in strength, which the slashes help combat.
It also lacked a downside that felt right and unique, incorporating poison into the geas helps with that a lot I think
